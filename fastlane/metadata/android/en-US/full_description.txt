Iridium is an EPUB reader that gets out of the way.

Read EPUBs already on your device. Point Iridium at a folder and your shelf fills in with covers, search, sorts and a continue-reading shelf — your files stay exactly where they are, nothing is copied or uploaded.

READING
• Paged and scrolled flow
• Per-book themes: light, sepia, grey, dark and pure black for AMOLED
• Text size, alignment, brightness and volume-key paging
• Immersive full-screen mode with a progress slider

HIGHLIGHTS AND NOTES
• Highlight in six colours and attach a note
• Jump back to any highlight from the book's details
• Bookmarks for the places you want to return to

SEARCH
• Full-text search inside your books, not just titles
• Results show the sentence around each match and open straight to that chapter

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Everything stays on your device and works fully offline
• Crash reporting is opt-in and nothing is ever sent automatically

Iridium hosts zero content — it only reads EPUB files you already own.
